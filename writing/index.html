Gatekeeping a Frontier LLM with a 66M-Parameter Model

How Sage decides when to speak: three weeks of moving the FIRE/SILENT decision from an LLM to a distilled DistilBERT. The labels were wrong before the model was, a silence timer was hiding 91% of the transcript, and distilling the arbiter made precision free. Includes a CPU microbenchmark, the cost math, and the honest gap between offline 90/90 and live P0.84/R0.69.
